methodologyHeading: "How We Build Every Guide"
methodology:
  - "**We visit in person.** Every hot spring on this site has been visited at least once by our team. We photograph, measure, and document conditions on-site."
  - "**We record hard data.** GPS coordinates, road surface conditions, actual water temperature ranges, parking capacity, cell coverage, and fee structures — not estimates from outdated forums."
  - "**We test seasonally.** Springs change dramatically between winter and summer. We revisit high-traffic locations across seasons to keep recommendations accurate."
  - "**We interview locals.** Rangers, campground hosts, regular soakers, and resort staff all contribute intel that never makes it into brochure copy."
  - "**We update continuously.** Guides are reviewed quarterly. If a road washes out, a fee changes, or a spring closes, the guide is updated within 48 hours of verification."

verificationHeading: "How Conditions Are Verified"
verification:
  - "**Primary source: on-site observation.** Temperatures are confirmed with contact thermometers or resort-reported data. Road conditions are assessed during the actual drive, not from satellite imagery."
  - "**Secondary source: land managers.** We cross-check access status with USFS ranger districts, BLM field offices, and Colorado State Parks before publishing."
  - "**Tertiary source: community reports.** We monitor visitor feedback, local social media groups, and seasonal closure alerts to catch changes between our visits."
  - "**Date stamps on every guide.** Each post shows a published date and, when applicable, a last-updated date. Outdated guides are flagged for review."

Soak Colorado was created to solve a specific problem: the internet is full of hot springs "guides" that copy the same ten locations from each other, get the water temperatures wrong, and never mention that the road has been closed since last spring.

This site takes a different approach. Every guide is the product of a real visit, written by someone who has actually driven the road, walked the trail, and soaked in the pool. We don't syndicate content, we don't use AI to generate descriptions, and we don't pad our lists with springs we haven't seen.

If you're planning a trip, you deserve better than recycled fluff. You deserve current conditions, honest pros and cons, and the kind of detail that only comes from standing on-site with a thermometer and a notebook.

That's what we deliver.
